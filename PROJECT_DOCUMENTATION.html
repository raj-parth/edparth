5. Interactive Mascot 'PARTH' & Zero-Cost Storage

Parth Mascot: Smoothly follows cursor movement across the screen. When the student clicks into any password box, Parth automatically enters Smart Password Privacy Mode and covers his eyes with his hands!

Zero-Cost Storage Strategy: Keeps Firebase completely free on the Spark tier by hosting heavy PDFs and question banks on the official Telegram channel (t.me/edparthbooks) and Google Drive.

6. DRM Security & Architectural Sign-Off

EdParth features a 4-layer DRM defense: context-menu right-click blocker, text selection prevention (user-select: none), DevTools keyboard shortcuts interception (F12, Ctrl+Shift+I, Ctrl+U), and dynamic student email watermarking.
